# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or perspective.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive community include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language or actions.
- Personal attacks, trolling, insults, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Any conduct that maintainers reasonably consider inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, events, and any public or private communication made on behalf of the project.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the project team at security@runwitness.dev. If that address is not configured, contact the project maintainers through the repository.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and privately. Maintainers will take reasonable steps to protect the privacy and safety of anyone who reports an incident.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior and may take appropriate action in response to violations, including warnings, removal of comments, temporary restrictions, or permanent bans from project spaces.

Enforcement decisions will be made in good faith and applied consistently, with the goal of protecting the health and safety of the project community.
